# Consent form: human annotators (SwissCivicQA-4L)

SwissCivicQA-4L contains **no data collected from data subjects**. All test items are written by the
dataset authors and grounded in the official text of the Swiss Federal Constitution (public domain,
Art. 5 para. 1 lit. a URG). The only human-generated data are **annotation labels** produced while
auditing the LLM judge. This form covers those labels.

By signing, the annotator confirms that:

1. They participate voluntarily and may withdraw at any time before publication; withdrawn labels are
   deleted and the judge-human agreement is recomputed without them.
2. Their labels (CORRECT / INCORRECT / NOT_ATTEMPTED per item) and the time of annotation are published
   as part of the dataset under **CDLA-Permissive-2.0**, linked only to a **pseudonymous ID** (e.g. `A1`).
   No name, e-mail address or other personal data is published.
3. **AI use:** they agree that their labels may be used to evaluate, train or improve AI systems,
   including use by third parties under the dataset licence.
4. They are at least 18 years old.

Withdrawal requests before publication: contact via the GitHub repository issues of the submitting team.
